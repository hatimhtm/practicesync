How it works: for each patient on the Practice Fusion dashboard, PracticeSync reads the name, date, and diagnosing doctor, finds which main doctor they fall under, then creates the appointment in SimplePractice on the same date with that doctor's codes — and saves it.

Doctors & Codes

Your big (main) doctors each hold several small doctors nested under them. When a patient is seen by a small doctor, the visit is filed under that small doctor's big doctor — with the small doctor's codes.

Big (main) doctors

Each big doctor has a 2-letter code that's added to every appointment (e.g. Caryn = GP, Heather = GO, Karine = GN).

Set up PracticeSync

4 quick steps. It reads each visit from Practice Fusion and books it in SimplePractice for you.

⚠️ First, quit Google Chrome so the app can use the browser you're already signed into.

👩‍⚕️

Step 1 — Your doctors

Load your doctor list, then review it and press Save.

🩺

Step 2 — Practice Fusion

Open the Practice Fusion home / search page in Chrome, paste its address, and press the button. A blue banner will ask you, in order, to: click the search box and type any patient's name, click that patient in the results, then click the patient name at the top, one visit row, the doctor, and the date. After this, you'll only need to type a name — no more addresses.

not taught
📆

Step 3 — SimplePractice

Open the SimplePractice New Appointment page, paste its address, and press the button. Click each field the banner asks for, then Save.

not taught
🔎

Step 4 — Check it

Type a patient's name, then pull their visits and make sure the doctor and date look right. Nothing is booked — this only reads. Then press Finish.
